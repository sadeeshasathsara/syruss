# Reflecting on recent work

A scheduled reflection has no document. Look at what happened since the last one: the corrections
people made (`review_history`), the quality figures in the briefing, and your memories.

- Turn repeated corrections into precise lessons (`remember`, kind `lesson`, with importance matching
  how costly the mistake was).
- Correct or retire memories the corrections prove wrong (`update_memory`).
- When a field is corrected again and again because the template can't hold it properly, propose the
  template change (read the `template-design` guide first).
- Use `query_records` to check a pattern across saved records before you write a lesson about it.

Don't repeat lessons you already have, and don't write a lesson from a single case unless a person
stated it as a rule. End with a short summary of what you learned.

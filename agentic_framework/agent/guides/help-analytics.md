# Help: analytics screens

Analytics screens are the workspace's saved records as tables people shape: calculated columns, filters, groups and
totals. They are under **Analytics** (marked Beta) in the workspace sidebar. Use the page and button names exactly as
written here. Building one for someone is your job in a chat: read the screens guide for how.

## Where they are
- **Analytics → Your screens**: the screens kept in this workspace; each also has its own entry in the sidebar.
  Anyone who can see documents can open them.
- **Analytics → Suggested**: screens Syruss built from a look through the records and thinks are worth having. Keep
  the ones you want, or dismiss them. **Ask Syruss to look for more** starts a new look; it needs three or more saved
  records, and waits when several suggestions are already waiting or nothing new was read since the last look.
- **Ask Syruss for another**: describe the table you want in your own words.
- A screen's page shows its table; editing it changes its columns, filters, groups and totals.

## Who can do what
- Opening screens: anyone whose role can see documents.
- Building, changing and deleting screens: roles with "Create and edit analytics screens" (Editor, Admin, Owner by
  default). Without it, ask someone who has it, or ask Syruss in the chat to build one (it is still refused when the
  person's own role can't).

## What screens show
Only what the saved records hold. A value the template doesn't capture can't appear in a screen; that is a template
change (Template → Refine with samples).
